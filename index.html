How to use this app

  • Record: tap Start. Use Pause/Resume if you need a break, Stop when you're done.
  • Fast vs Accurate: Fast transcribes quicker but with more mistakes; Accurate is slower but more precise. Pick with the toggle at the top — it's remembered next time, and loads quietly in the background so it's usually ready before you need it.
  • Numbers: after transcribing, numbers said more than once show green ("Confirmed"); numbers said only once show amber ("Verify") — those are worth double-checking against the playback.
  • Playback: use the audio player under the message box to listen back to what you actually said.
  • Transcript vs Message: the transcript is read-only — exactly what was transcribed. Edit the message box instead; that's what gets shared or saved, and it saves automatically as you type.
  • Photo / Video: tap "Add Photo" or "Add Video" to open your camera and attach it to this note. Video (like the audio recording) is only kept for the current session — not saved into Previous Notes.
  • Share: sends the message plus whichever of photo/video/audio are attached, via WhatsApp, Mail, Drive, Dropbox, or whatever's installed. Works from the main screen or directly from a note in Previous Notes.
  • Save: downloads the message as a text file, plus the photo, video, and recording for whichever are attached.
  • Previous Notes: search, browse past notes, share or delete individual ones, export/share your whole history as a backup, import a backup back in, or clear everything.
