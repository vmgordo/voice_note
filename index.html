How to use this app

  • Record: tap Start. Use Pause/Resume if you need a break, Stop when you're done.
  • Fast vs Accurate: Fast transcribes quicker but with more mistakes; Accurate is slower but more precise. Pick with the toggle at the top — it's remembered next time, and that model loads quietly in the background so it's usually ready before you need it.
  • Numbers: after transcribing, numbers said more than once show green ("Confirmed"); numbers said only once show amber ("Verify") — those are the ones worth double-checking.
  • Playback: use the audio player under the record buttons to listen back to what you actually said, especially for amber numbers.
  • Edit: fix anything in the title, transcript, or message boxes any time — your changes save automatically as you type.
  • Photo / Video: tap "Add Photo" or "Add Video" to open your camera. If you're mid-recording, this pauses and safely saves what's been captured so far — the microphone automatically reconnects and recording continues once you're back, no audio lost either way.
  • Share: sends the message plus photo and video (whichever are attached) via WhatsApp, Mail, Google Drive, Dropbox, or whatever's installed. Works from the main screen or directly from a note in Previous Notes. The audio recording isn't included in Share (some apps reject the whole attachment when it's mixed in) — use Save to get the audio file.
  • Save: downloads the message as a text file, plus the photo, video, and original recording for whichever are attached (video and recording are only available right after you capture them, not for reopened past notes).
  • Previous Notes: search, browse past notes by date, export your whole history as a backup file, share that backup file (e.g. straight to Drive/Dropbox), import a backup back in, or clear everything.
